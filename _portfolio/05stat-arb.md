excerpt: "Implemented the Avellaneda-Lee (2010) statistical arbitrage framework on a universe of 40 cryptocurrencies using hourly data over a full year. Built a rolling-PCA pipeline to extract eigenportfolios, modeled the residuals as an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, and converted the resulting s-scores into a market-neutral trading signal. The first eigenportfolio tracked broad market moves while the second captured a more stable cross-sectional structure; the resulting long/short strategy delivered a modest positive Sharpe ratio (~0.08) with a tightly controlled drawdown (~2.5%), prioritizing risk control over aggressive return-seeking."
