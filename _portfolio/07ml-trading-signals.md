excerpt: "Built an end-to-end pipeline &mdash; data cleaning, technical-indicator feature engineering, and SVM/Random Forest classifiers &mdash; to predict next-day stock direction, then converted predictions into trading signals and backtested them with Backtrader. Validated the approach on AAPL before scaling it to a broad equity universe, ranking stocks by out-of-sample accuracy and backtesting the top 10. The results showed that classification accuracy alone doesn't guarantee trading quality: stocks with similar accuracy had very different Sharpe ratios and drawdowns, with steadier-trending names delivering the strongest risk-adjusted performance."
