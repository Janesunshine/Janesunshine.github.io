excerpt: "Built and compared predictive models &mdash; decision trees, clustering, linear/polynomial/logistic regression, and bagged ensembles &mdash; to forecast hourly bike-share demand from ~17,400 records of weather, time, and calendar features. The best model (bagging, mtry=11, ntree=1000) achieved the lowest test error of everything we tried, and identified hour-of-day and weekend status as the strongest demand drivers. Translated the analysis into operational recommendations: GPS-based fleet rebalancing and time-targeted promotions during peak hours. Team project."
